Our Professional Services

From residential cleaning to fumigation, garden care, and property management — we deliver reliable, hygienic, and professional solutions you can trust.

Explore Services

Residential Cleaning

We provide a complete cleaning solution that covers every area of your home, ensuring a spotless and hygienic environment. With careful attention to detail and high cleaning standards, we help maintain a clean, calm, and comfortable space you’ll always feel good coming back to.

Our residential cleaning covers deep cleaning, routine maintenance, post-construction cleaning, stain removal, and sanitation. We use eco-friendly products that are safe for children and pets while ensuring spotless results.

Fumigation & Pest Control

Our pest control services combine advanced treatment methods with safe application practices to deliver powerful, long-lasting results. We help protect your home and workplace from harmful pests while maintaining a healthy, comfortable space.

We eliminate termites, cockroaches, rodents, mosquitoes, ants, and more using approved chemicals that are safe for residential and commercial spaces, ensuring a pest-free environment.

Office & Commercial Cleaning

Our office cleaning services are designed to create a healthy and presentable work environment. From daily maintenance to deep cleaning, we ensure every workspace remains clean, comfortable, and conducive to employee well-being and performance.

We help maintain a healthy and productive work environment with scheduled cleaning, waste management, sanitation, and customized solutions for corporate buildings and facilities.

Property Management

We offer professional property management services to help clients buy, rent, and manage properties with ease and confidence. Our solutions are reliable, transparent, and client-focused.

Our services cover properties for sale and rent, featured listings, and direct support through experienced agents. We handle the details so you can enjoy stress-free property transactions and management.

Estimated Cost: ₦100,000 – ₦5,000,000+

Garden Services

Our garden services are designed to enhance the beauty, health, and value of your outdoor spaces. We provide professional care to keep your garden neat, vibrant, and well-maintained all year round.

Our services include lawn care and maintenance, weed control, hedge trimming, and complete landscape cleanup. Whether for residential or commercial properties, we tailor our solutions to suit your outdoor needs.

Estimated Cost: ₦40,000 – ₦1,000,000,000
